The Problem

Trades Training Hasn't Changed in Decades

Students watch videos and read manuals — but get little real-time feedback during hands-on practice. Dangerous habits form undetected. Too many students never finish. The industry loses billions to callbacks, rework, and workplace accidents.

No Real-Time Correction

A student can practice a dangerous wiring technique for weeks before an instructor catches it.

One Instructor, Many Students

Instructors can't watch every student at once. Critical feedback moments get missed.

Completion Rates

A large share of trade school students don't finish their program — often for want of personalized support at the moment they need it.

A Widening Labor Gap

Skilled positions are going unfilled faster than training programs can replace retiring tradespeople. Current systems can't close that gap alone.

How It Works

AI-Powered Training, in Real Time

TradeVision AI runs on consumer AR headsets like the Meta Quest 3. The student works with real tools in the real world — the AI watches, guides, and corrects alongside them.

1

Student Puts on the Headset

Using a Meta Quest 3, the student enters their training environment with the AI instructor active.

2

AI Watches in Real Time

Computer vision identifies tools, components, and actions as the student works — detecting errors before they become habits.

3

Instant Guidance and Scoring

Step-by-step overlays, safety warnings, and performance scores appear in the student's field of view — like a master tradesperson watching over their shoulder.
